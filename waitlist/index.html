Buy and sell on campus, one day at a time.

A marketplace just for UArizona students — clothes, shoes, dorm stuff, books and tech, handed off in person on campus.

How it works

  1. 1
    List it for 24 hours

    A photo, a price, and when you can meet. Listings don't linger — what you see is what's actually still around.

  2. 2
    Buyers book an exact time and spot

    No "is this still available?" Pick a 30-minute slot at a campus meetup spot from the times the seller already offered.

  3. 3
    Make an offer

    Price too high? Offer less, with the time and place attached. The seller accepts or declines.

What Dibbs is not

Dibbs doesn't hold your money, handle payment, or check who anyone is. A campus email proves someone is a student here — not that they're trustworthy. Meet in public, on campus, and use your own judgment.

Dibbs is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Arizona.
