Buy and sell on campus, one day at a time.

A marketplace just for UArizona students — clothes, shoes, dorm stuff, books and tech, handed off in person on campus.

    List it for 24 hours

    A photo, a price, and when you can meet. Listings don't linger.

  2. 2
    Buyers book an exact time and spot

    No "is this still available?" — pick a 30-minute slot at a campus meetup spot.

  3. 3
    Make an offer

    Price too high? Offer less. The seller accepts or declines.
