# Flyer and QR

QR link: `https://trydibbs.com/?utm_source=flyer&utm_medium=print&utm_campaign=launch`
Use a different `utm_source` suffix per location if you want to compare spots
(for example `flyer-laundry`). The site does not store UTM values yet (see
README), so track by link shortener.

## Three headline and body options

**A. Plain**
Headline: Sell your stuff to other students.
Body: List it. Agree on a price. Meet on campus. Pay each other in person.

**B. Move-out**
Headline: Don't throw it out. Sell it.
Body: Fridge, lamp, textbooks, desk chair. Another student probably needs it.
Take a photo and list it on Dibbs.

**C. Dibbs offer** (only once staff are ready and hours are set)
Headline: Don't want to list it? Ask Dibbs to buy it.
Body: Send photos. A person on our team sends an estimate. You get a final
offer after we see the item. You can say no.

## QR caption

Scan to see what's for sale on campus.
(Alt for C: Scan to send photos and get an estimate.)

## Tear-off tab line

Dibbs. Used stuff, on campus. trydibbs.com

## Small print for the bottom

Not run by or affiliated with the university. Students pay each other in
person. Dibbs does not handle payments.

## Where to post

Posting on or near campus (dorm boards, laundry rooms, the student union,
shared bulletin boards, residence halls, off-campus windows) often needs
permission or follows rules about where flyers can go and how long they stay.
**Check the university's posting policy and ask the building or board owner
before you post anywhere.** This kit does not state what the policy says.
Take flyers down when the campaign ends.
