unsubscribe line from `launch-email.md` if it is marketing. Check with the
lawyer whether account emails like these count as marketing.

## Day 2: list your first item

**Subject:** Got something you don't use?

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Look around your room. A desk lamp, a textbook, a jacket you stopped wearing.
Someone on campus might want it.

Take a few clear photos in good light. Say what it is and what shape it's in.
Set a fair price. Listings stay in Browse for 24 hours.

Rather not list it? You can ask Dibbs to make you an offer instead.

## Day 6: safety and meetups

**Subject:** Before your first meetup

Hi [FIRST NAME],

A few quick habits for meeting up.

Meet in a public, well-lit place on campus. Go in daytime if you can. Bring a
friend. Check the item before you pay. Leave if something feels off.

You can report any listing, post or user in the app, and you can block anyone.
In an emergency, call 911.
