# Dibbs: one-pager

## What it is
Dibbs is a marketplace for used stuff, built for students buying and selling
from each other on campus.

## Who it's for
Students who want to sell things they no longer need, and students who want a
fair price on used items without travelling far.

## How it works
1. **List it.** Add photos, a short description and a price.
2. **Agree.** Chat and pick a spot and time on campus.
3. **Meet.** Check the item in person.
4. **Pay each other.** Cash or whatever you two agree on, outside the app.

Prefer not to list? Use "Get a Dibbs offer." Send photos. A person on the team
reviews them and sends an estimate. The final offer comes after we inspect the
item. Either side can say no. Payment is arranged outside the app.

## What it does not do
- It does not handle payments, hold money or protect a payment.
- It does not check anyone's identity. A "campus email verified" badge only
  means someone confirmed an email address.
- It is not run by or affiliated with the university.
- It does not guarantee safety. Meet in public, bring a friend, check items
  before you pay.

## Safety and rules
Anyone can report a listing, post or user and block a user in the app. A person
reviews reports. Some items are not allowed, such as weapons, alcohol, drugs
and stolen goods. See the Community rules.
