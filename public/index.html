Dibbs: buy and sell used stuff at the University of Arizona

Dibbs is a campus marketplace for UArizona students. Sell the mini fridge, desk chair, textbooks or bike you don't need, or pick up what you do, from students nearby.

How it works

  1. List an item with a few photos and an asking price. It stays up for 24 hours.
  2. A buyer makes an offer or books a time and a campus meetup spot.
  3. You meet, hand it off, and both confirm it's done.

Good for

Move-in and move-out, dorm and apartment furniture, used textbooks, bikes and scooters, electronics, clothes, and game day gear around Tucson.
